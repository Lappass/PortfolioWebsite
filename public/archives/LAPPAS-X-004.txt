FILE X-004 / Last Mile
A DELIVERY STATION MANAGEMENT GAME

Role: Solo developer · Indie
Date: 2026
Stack: Unity · C#
Status: PUBLIC

A ten-day management game about running a neighbourhood delivery station. Each wave of orders lands on a live 3D city as countdown pins, and you decide who takes them: riders, robots or drones. Speed is only half the job; heavy loads, stairs, fatigue and the riders' own lives all weigh on every assignment.

Highlights
1. Real-time dispatch on a 3D city: routing modes, traffic, rush hours, fatigue and heavy loads all change who should take an order.
2. A small cast of riders with their own stories, written in a dry corporate voice, and choices that carry from one day to the next.
3. A procedurally laid-out city with a day-night cycle and automated play-through checks in the editor; the illustrations and landmark models are AI-generated and assembled by editor scripts.
