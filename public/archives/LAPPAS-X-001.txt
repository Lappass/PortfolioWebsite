Portfolio Site
This website

Shuhang Chen · Solo, based on RhineLabUI · TypeScript / Three.js / Vite / Blender · 2026

The works are shown as game cases on a shelf. Pick one and press Enter: the figure carries the disc to the console, the monitor reads it and the work's page opens. The site is built on RhineLabUI, an open-source 3D archive interface by LBEILC, which I kept for the shelf and the drag controls. The cases, console, monitor, figure animations and boot sequence are my additions, modelled with Blender scripts.

- The shelf, the glass materials and the drag controls come from RhineLabUI.
- The game case, console and monitor are generated by Blender scripts in art/, so the models can be rebuilt from source.
- All of the upstream project's IP references were removed. The music and sound effects are synthesized in code.
