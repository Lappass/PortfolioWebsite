FILE X-002 / LOCKDOWN
A HOUSEHOLD SURVIVAL GAME

Role: Solo developer · Indie
Date: 2026
Stack: Unity · C#
Status: PUBLIC

A household survival game set during a pandemic lockdown. Each day you open the delivery, read messages from family and the news, keep the fridge in order, and cook for everyone at home. Cooking is a spatial puzzle: cut ingredients into shapes and fit them into the pan, tangram style.

Highlights
1. Cooking as a tangram-like puzzle: cut, rotate and fit ingredient pieces into the pan.
2. Fridge storage as a grid inventory where food spoils if left too long.
3. Daily loop of deliveries, family messages and news that shape what the household needs.

DESIGN INTENT / Where the rules come from

LockDown is about slowly losing control while you wait for something with no announced end. The idea comes from my family's 2022 lockdown in Shanghai. We ate whatever came in the supply package, my grandparents needed medicine that nobody was allowed to go and get, and nobody told us when it would end.

I use those memories as design rules. The player receives supplies instead of shopping. The apartment never grows. Food, medicine and the neighbors' requests all come out of one small reserve. The run lasts twenty days, but the screen shows the day number with no countdown.

The cooking puzzle starts from the traditional Chinese tangram. Here the shapes are ingredients and the space is a pan or a fridge, and the gaps and leftovers show the shortage without a line of dialogue.

CORE LOOP / One day, one meal

Each morning starts with yesterday's consequences. Everyone loses 2 morale and 1 health, the fridge is checked for rot, and a package arrives. By Day 7 the bag can be just noodles, a cabbage and some rice.

The player reads the phone, plans the meal, fits the food into the pan and serves it. Leftovers go into the fridge if they fit and are lost if they don't. Before Day 3 there is no fridge, and only the largest leftover keeps on the counter. Sleep moves the day on but resets nothing.

COOKING / A pan that is never big enough

I built the game around a pan rather than a resource screen, because every problem in the house ends up at dinner. A resource screen would turn Grandpa's tablets into a number. The pan turns them into a space that is too small.

The twelve ingredients are shapes of 3 to 7 cells, each with its own calories and nutrients. A cabbage is a five-cell cross worth 25 kcal, and pork takes seven cells and gives 250. A meal is scored against a 470 kcal target: 38% balance, 24% calories, 20% total nutrients and 18% pan filled. Under 42% nobody recovers health, and under 28% everyone gets weaker.

Until Day 6 there is no knife, so the pan is a plain rectangle and every piece goes in whole. After that the pan is an irregular shape of roughly 18 to 25 cells, always with a solid 3 by 3 core so the largest piece fits. The knife cuts along the grid, and the part you don't cook goes to the fridge.

FRIDGE AND SPOILAGE / Storage that turns into a chore

Each new rule arrives through something in the house. On Day 1 the fridge is broken, and the next day Aunt Li complains in the building group about Grandpa dragging it around at midnight. On Day 3 he gets it working and storage unlocks. After two days of cooking everything at once, the player knows what a fridge is for.

The fridge is a 6 by 8 grid. On Day 7 the building loses power, and from Day 8 anything stored for two days grows a rotten cell. Each morning a rotten cell spreads to the next cell of the same item 62% of the time and into a touching item 35% of the time.

Rot never deletes food. The good cells can still be cooked, or cut away from the bad ones. The grade ignores rot on purpose. Every rotten cell in the pan costs each person 4 health, up to 14 a meal, so an S meal with rot in it still makes the family ill.

NARRATIVE / Three at home, five in the building chat

In 2022 there were five of us at home. For the game I cut the household to three: Grandpa, whose health depends on things from outside, Daughter, who says out loud what everyone feels, and the player, with no name or face, who makes the decisions. Director Zhang, Mr. Wang, Aunt Li, Uncle Yu and the 6F Stranger reach the flat through the building chat.

Grandpa starts on 60 health and loses 5 more every morning until his medicine is secured. On Day 5 his last box is empty. Aunt Li checks her own drawer, and half the time her strip is empty too. The 6F Stranger can get the pills sooner for two things from the fridge, and the chat names the two the game took.

On Day 8 Daughter has a fever of 38.2. A warm meal from a fridge item gives her 25 health, and letting her rest costs her 20. On Day 13 Zhang asks for food for the sealed seventh floor. On Day 18 taking Daughter downstairs early works 60% of the time, and if the patrol stops them, everyone loses 12 morale.

INFORMATION / The phone and the screen

The phone has three channels at three distances: the city newspaper, the building group and the family chat. Often they cover the same problem. On Day 4 Zhang asks every flat for its medicine doses, on Day 5 the paper reports refills running late, and that morning Grandpa finds his box empty.

The HUD shows the day and each person's health. After dinner one family member answers in a single line, the weakest one when the meal went badly, and that is how the player learns what the family thinks of the food. A meal with rot in it gets "That tastes off. Is it still fresh?" The letter grade sits behind a small Last meal toggle. On Day 20 the lockdown simply ends, and the report shows the days at home and each person's health.

NEXT BUILD / What I'd build next

In the next build the knife slowly goes blunt. Hard ingredients wear it down faster, a blunt knife only cuts soft food like tofu or cabbage, and sharpening it depends on a neighbor lending a stone. Cut surfaces spoil faster than whole ingredients. On outage days the spoilage clock advances twice and the fridge hum cuts out.

Choices should also reach further. In that build the player picks which two items go to the 6F Stranger, and he comes back on Day 13 expecting the same deal. If Daughter was left to rest, her fever returns on Day 14. 7F sends something back on Day 17 only if they were helped. If the patrol catches the family on Day 18, no package arrives on Day 19.
