# Sniff & Snitch art sources

Inputs for `art/sniff_poster.mjs` (work X-004). All of it is the Sniff & Snitch team's own art.

- `lyra.png`, `fia.png`: one figure each, cut out of the team's final colour sheet of the two leads (Lyra in her black-and-plum outfit, Fia with goggles, wrench and tail; November 2026, replacing the earlier greyscale/first-variant sheets). The flat grey background (#898989) was flooded out from the border, enclosed pure-grey pockets removed, the edge pulled in by a pixel, and the sheet's palette swatches left out.
- `placard-lyra.png`, `placard-fia.png`: the booking placards from the game's character select, copied unchanged from `Assets/Sniff/art/2d/ui/SelectionUI/Lyra.png` and `Fia.png` in the game project.
