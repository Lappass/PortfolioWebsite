# Sniff & Snitch art sources

Inputs for `art/sniff_poster.mjs` (work X-004). All of it is the Sniff & Snitch team's own art.

- `lyra.png`, `fia.png`: one figure each, cut out of the team's character sheets (Lyra: the first of the three greyscale outfit variants; Fia: the first colour variant). The flat grey background was flooded out from the crop's border and the edge pulled in by a pixel.
- `placard-lyra.png`, `placard-fia.png`: the booking placards from the game's character select, copied unchanged from `Assets/Sniff/art/2d/ui/SelectionUI/Lyra.png` and `Fia.png` in the game project.
